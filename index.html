FAQ

Common questions, before applying.

How is this different from the podcast, the IG content, and the free workbook?

The IG content is the daily cadence. The podcast is the long-form conversation. The workbook is the surface introduction. Crypto Made Easy, the Freedom Circle, and the 1:1 are the install. Same framework, turned into modules, weekly cadence, structured trust curriculum, and operator work you can run on a Monday.

Foundation, cohort, or bespoke. Which tier is for me?

Foundation if your position is under $50K and you're building the entity layer plus the mindset work that lets you hold through the cycle. Cohort if your position is $50K to $250K and you want the structural year with cohort accountability. Bespoke if your position is $250K and beyond and you want the full sequence run alongside the operator. The diagnostic routes you in ninety seconds.

Is this personalized tax or financial advice?

No. OTO is an educator. He is not a registered tax advisor or financial advisor. The curriculum is educational. Your own licensed tax professional or financial advisor handles personalized application of any structure discussed. See compliance footer.

How fast will I see results?

The framework is structural and long-game. The foundation curriculum installs the entity layer and mindset work inside the first 30 days. The cohort installs the structural year over 10 weeks. The bespoke 1:1 runs the full sequence over 6-12 months. Crypto returns vary by cycle and holder; results are not typical.

What's the time commitment?

Crypto Made Easy is self-paced. Crypto Freedom Circle is 10 weeks with weekly group calls. The 1:1 is bespoke, typically 6-12 months. The trust + common law module (MasterHeart) inside Circle is 10 additional weeks of advanced curriculum.

How does the investment work across the tiers?

Stage-specific and confirmed at intake. Crypto Made Easy has public pricing on its own page. Crypto Freedom Circle and the 1:1 Mentorship are application-only because the fit and stage conversation matters more than the figure at those tiers.

Where are you based?

California. Born in Brazil. Cohorts and 1:1 engagements are global. Pacific time zone for live calls.

What if I'm not ready for any tier yet?

Download the free workbook and the 7-tax-structures guide. Listen to a few podcast episodes. The framework will be here when the position size or the stage fits. No pressure on the timeline.
