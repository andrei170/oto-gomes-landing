THE PROBLEM

The Year-One Tax Trap
Most Crypto Investors Hit

Trade rebalances, wallet transfers, swaps. Every one is a taxable event most investors never track until April.

TRAP 01

Basis Tracking Errors

Exchange reports do not match wallet activity. By year-end the spreadsheet is a mess and the CPA bill triples trying to reconcile it.

TRAP 02

Short-Term Gain Surprise

Rebalances inside a 12-month window trigger short-term capital gains, taxed as ordinary income. Most investors discover this after filing.

TRAP 03

Wallet-To-Wallet Missteps

Some wallet moves are taxable events, some are not. The difference is structural. Most guides and most CPAs get it wrong.
